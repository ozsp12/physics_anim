## Interactive mechanics

### Roller-coaster vertical loop

Path: [`rollercoster_loop/`](rollercoster_loop/)

A point mass starts from rest at a selectable height, descends a smooth ramp, and enters a vertical circular loop. The interface exposes the release height \(h\), loop radius \(R\), ratio \(h/R\), speed, normal force per unit mass, energy partition, predicted regime, trajectory, and force vectors.

The simulation distinguishes three principal regimes:

1. \(h/R \leq 1\): the particle loses its kinetic energy while still supported by the track and returns;
2. \(1 < h/R < 5/2\): the normal reaction becomes zero and the particle enters free flight;
3. \(h/R \geq 5/2\): the particle can complete the loop while maintaining nonnegative contact force.
